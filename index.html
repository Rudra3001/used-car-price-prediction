Indian Valuation Engine — Official Emblem

CARPRICE AI (v4.2)

Autonomous valuation platform engineered for India's automobile market across 10 top OEMs, 43 passenger models, a 2006–2026 manufacturing spectrum, and leakage-free algorithmic regression inference.

database

10,000 Verified Records

Dataset spanning 10 brands, 43 models, and years 2006 to 2026. Features include mileage, engine capacity, ownership counts, service logs, and accident claim history.

security

Leakage-Free 80:20 Split

StandardScaler and OneHotEncoder were fitted exclusively on 8,000 training cars before transforming the 2,000 test cars, preventing feature leakage and optimistic bias.

currency_rupee

Indian Rupee (₹ INR) Engine

Dynamic conversion into Indian Lakhs (₹ 18.25 Lakh) and full INR amounts (₹ 18,24,475), with confidence intervals from the model's test RMSE (± ₹ 55,579).
